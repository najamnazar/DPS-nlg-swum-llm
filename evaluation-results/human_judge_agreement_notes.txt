======================================================================
2. What was compared
======================================================================

- Judge: meta-llama/llama-4-maverick, temperature 0, default corpora
  (evaluation-results/fact_preservation_verdicts.csv). The annotator never saw
  the judge's labels, evidence or justifications.
- Unit: one fact x one summary. 141 facts x 4 summaries = 564 label pairs,
  each Preserved / Omitted / Incorrect.
- Join: on (project, pattern, file, tool, fact_id); all 564 matched and the
  fact texts are identical in the workbook and the judge output.
- Statistics:
  - % agreement and Cohen's kappa, for the three labels and for
    Preserved vs not Preserved (binary).
  - Gwet's AC1: like kappa, but not pulled down when one label dominates
    (e.g. SWUM, where 91% of human labels are Omitted).
  - 95% CIs: bootstrap over the 30 files (2,000 resamples), because labels
    within a file are not independent.
  - Score level: each summary's preserved rate (share of its facts labelled
    Preserved), human vs judge: Spearman, Pearson, ICC(A,1) (absolute agreement).
  - Ranking: the order of the four tools within each file, Kendall's tau-b and
    the share of tool pairs ordered the same way (as in the TOSEM analysis).
- Script: python/human_judge_agreement.py
  Full figures: the HUMAN-JUDGE AGREEMENT block of evaluation-results/results.txt and the
  human_judge_agreement_*.csv files (labels, metrics, confusion, summaries,
  tools, per_file, annotator_only).

4. Where they disagree: the judge is more lenient
======================================================================

Confusion matrix (rows = judge, columns = human)
                 Preserved  Omitted  Incorrect   total
  Preserved            258       74          1     333
  Omitted                1      218          4     223
  Incorrect              2        1          5       8
  total                261      293         10     564

- 75 of the 83 disagreements are the judge saying Preserved where the human
  did not; only 3 go the other way (McNemar p < .001).
- When the human says Preserved, the judge agrees 99% of the time; when the
  judge says Preserved, the human agrees 77% of the time.
- Both raters rarely use Incorrect (judge 8, human 10) and they match on 5.
  Incorrect labels are too few to evaluate separately.

Causes, from the annotator's notes and the judge's evidence:
1. "X overrides method m" (36 pairs; 22 of them NLG). NLG summaries list the
   methods ("The 3 methods of BossVisitor are visitEngineer ..."). The judge
   counts the listing as Preserved; the annotator requires the summary to say
   the method overrides/implements an inherited one.
2. Pattern roles without the partner class (10 pairs; 8 SWUM). "MacOSFactory
   acts as a factory in the Abstract Factory pattern" was accepted by the judge
   for "MacOSFactory is a concrete factory for GUIFactory"; the annotator
   followed the prompt's rule that naming the pattern without linking the
   specific classes is Omitted.
3. Loose paraphrases in LLM summaries (inheritance 10, constructor 3, field 3,
   "defines" 5). E.g. "with bean-name awareness" accepted for "implements
   BeanNameAware"; "including parent ... information" for "defines
   visitParentName".
4. Vague SWUM method mentions ("provides methods to create, create, create")
   accepted for a specific create method (7 "defines" pairs).
5. Remaining 9 pairs: SWUM calls an interface a "class" (2, human Incorrect);
   NLG's "inherited by EmailNotificationListener, LogOpenListener" judged
   Incorrect for the concrete-observer roles but accepted by the human (2);
   5 single cases.

The 2026-10-27 notes listed a further cause (14 interface "defines" pairs the
annotator labelled Incorrect). The annotator has since re-marked those facts
Correct in the code and relabelled the summaries, so it no longer applies.

Effect on the paper's numbers: the judge's preserved rates are inflated by
about 10-15 points (NLG +15, SWUM +13, Mistral +13, GPT +10), but the
ordering of the tools is the same as the annotator's.

5. Annotator-only findings (no judge counterpart)
======================================================================

Fact extraction (code check of the 141 facts)
- 135 Correct, 6 Incorrect (96% correct).
- The 6 are real extraction errors: Flower (F19) given Abstract Factory and
  Observer roles and "extends Plant" (implements) and a private constructor
  (public); Mouse (F27) "extends ComputerPart" (implements);
  LazyInitializationDirector (F25) INSTANCE field belongs to the nested holder
  class. Agreement on these facts: 92% over 24 labels.

Unsupported claims about the pattern (summaries with "Yes", of 30)
  DPS_NLG 23   DPS_LLM (Mistral) 21   DPS_LLM (GPT) 15   DPS_SWUM 2

Extra facts the extracted facts miss (47 in 22 files: 24 method calls,
17 pattern roles, 3 fields, 3 constructors)
                       Preserved  Omitted  Incorrect
  DPS_NLG                      0       18         29
  DPS_LLM (GPT)                1       33         13
  DPS_LLM (Mistral)            5       28         14
  DPS_SWUM                     1       45          1
- Of the 17 extra pattern-role facts, the annotator labelled the summary
  Incorrect for NLG 17, Mistral 14, GPT 13, SWUM 1. The notes show these are
  summaries that deny any design pattern ("No explicit design pattern is
  applied") in classes whose code or Javadoc shows one.
- So the fact-based score does not capture wrong pattern statements: NLG ranks
  first on preserved facts but has the most unsupported claims and wrong
  pattern statements. Worth reporting next to the preservation rates.

6. For the paper
======================================================================

- The judge agrees with the annotator substantially (kappa 0.72, 85%) and
  ranks the tools in the same order (98% of pairs within files), so its
  ranking can be reported as validated.
- Its absolute preserved rates are optimistic: 59% Preserved vs 46% for the
  human on the same 564 pairs. Report them as upper bounds or give the human
  rates for the 30-file sample alongside.
- The leniency is greatest for NLG method lists and SWUM role sentences, so
  the gap between NLG and the LLMs is smaller under human labels (and
  reverses with macro averaging).
- One annotator only: this is judge-human agreement, not inter-annotator
  agreement.
